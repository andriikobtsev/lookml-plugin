3. The plugin

LookML Support runs locally in your IDE and edits local files.

  • No telemetry. The plugin opens no network connection to us and sends us no usage, file, project or code data. Your LookML never leaves your machine.
  • Local counters only. To decide when to show the occasional in-IDE notice, such as the one-time request for a Marketplace review, the plugin keeps a small counter in your IDE settings: how many LookML files you have opened, on how many separate days, and whether a notice was already shown. This stays on your machine and is never transmitted.
  • Licence checks are performed by the JetBrains licensing framework built into your IDE, not by us. Any communication with JetBrains servers takes place between your IDE and JetBrains.
  • Links you click. Actions such as "Write a review" open the JetBrains Marketplace in your normal browser. From that point JetBrains' privacy policy applies, exactly as if you had typed the address yourself.
  • Your IDE itself may collect its own diagnostics under JetBrains' own privacy policy, which we neither control nor receive.

We do not use your address for advertising and there is no newsletter.
